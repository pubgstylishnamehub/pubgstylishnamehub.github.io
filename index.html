How the generator works

Stylish PUBG names are not made with a special font file. They are built from Unicode — the giant library of characters that includes mathematical letters, circled letters, small caps and thousands of symbols. When you type in the box above, the generator swaps each ordinary letter for its matching fancy Unicode version and, for the decorated styles, wraps the result in crowns, wings, stars or brackets.

Because the output is plain text, it pastes anywhere text works: your PUBG Mobile profile, a clan tag, or another game or app. Everything happens in your browser — the name you type is never uploaded, stored or shared, and there is nothing to install and no account to create.

One thing worth knowing: every app renders Unicode with its own font, so an exotic character occasionally shows as an empty box on some devices. If that happens, pick a simpler style — the bold, script and small-caps versions are supported almost everywhere.

A good in-game name is short, easy to read in a kill feed and easy for teammates to say out loud. Two to eight letters is the sweet spot: it stays readable and leaves plenty of the 16-character budget for decoration. How to Change Your Name in PUBG Mobile

Your PUBG Mobile name appears everywhere: in the lobby, on the kill feed, on your clan page and next to every chicken dinner. The name you picked when you first installed the game may not suit you any more — maybe it was a rushed choice, maybe your clan wants matching tags, or maybe you have found a stylish version you like better. Changing it is quick, but it is not free-form: PUBG Mobile only lets you rename with a special item called a Rename Card. This guide covers where that card comes from, the exact steps to use it, and how to make sure your new name is accepted the first time.

What you need: a Rename Card

A Rename Card is a single-use item stored in your inventory. One card equals one name change, and the card is consumed the moment your new name is confirmed. Your very first name, chosen when you create your account, is free — every change after that needs a card. There is no way around this inside the game, so if a website or a stranger claims they can rename your account without one, they cannot; never hand your login details to anyone offering that.

Where to get a Rename Card

There are a few honest ways to get one:

  • Progress missions. As you level up your account, the progress mission track rewards a Rename Card at certain early levels. If you have never claimed yours, check the missions screen before spending anything.
  • The in-game shop. The Rename Card is sold in the shop's items section for UC. This is the fastest route if you need a name change today.
  • Events and crew challenges. Limited-time events and crew activities occasionally include a Rename Card among their rewards. It is worth a quick look at the events tab before buying one.

Once you have the card, it sits in your inventory until you use it — it does not expire while it waits, so there is no rush to decide on a name.

Step by step: applying your new name

  1. Open PUBG Mobile and, from the main lobby, tap Inventory at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap the box-shaped icon at the bottom of the inventory to open your stored items, and find the Rename Card.
  3. Select the card and tap Use. A text field appears where you can enter your new name.
  4. Type or paste your new name. If you prepared a stylish name with our stylish name generator, paste it here directly from your clipboard.
  5. Check the preview, then tap OK to confirm. Your Rename Card is used and the new name takes effect immediately.

Your friends list, clan page and profile update straight away. Note that your account ID number never changes — only the display name does — so your friends can always find you by ID even after a rename.

The naming rules to respect

PUBG Mobile checks every new name before accepting it. Names must be between 3 and 16 characters long, and every letter, number, symbol and space counts toward that total. Names must also be unique across the game, which is why adding a symbol or a clan tag often turns a taken name into an available one. Offensive words, impersonation of staff or developers, and anything caught by the game's filter will be rejected — and a name that slips through can still be reported later and force-changed, costing you the card. Finally, not every exotic character renders in the game's font; a character the game cannot display may appear as a box or cause the name to be refused.

Before you spend the card: a quick checklist

Because each change costs a card, it pays to prepare. First, draft your name in the generator on our home page and copy the exact style you want, rather than trying to type symbols from memory. Second, count the characters — decorated styles grow quickly, and a name over 16 characters will not fit; shorter base names leave more room for decoration, as explained in our guide to PUBG symbols. Third, read the name once more for typos and for anything that could read as offensive out of context. If you are still deciding what the name should say, the PUBG name ideas guide has cool, funny and pro-style suggestions to start from.

Common problems

If the game says the name is already in use, add a distinguishing touch — a number, a symbol at the end, or your clan tag in brackets. If it says the name contains invalid characters, one of your symbols is not supported; remove symbols one at a time, or fall back to a plainer font style such as bold or small caps, which are widely supported. And if you cannot find your Rename Card, make sure you are looking in the box section of the inventory rather than among your outfits and weapon skins.

With a card in hand and a name prepared, the whole change takes under a minute — and you will be dropping into your next match with a name that finally looks the part.

Best PUBG Name Ideas: Cool, Funny and Pro Styles

A great PUBG name does three jobs at once: it looks sharp in the kill feed, it is easy for teammates to call out in voice chat, and it still feels like you after a hundred matches. The ideas below are original starting points grouped by style. Pro-style names

Clan-style names

Clans usually share a format so their members are recognisable at a glance. Common patterns are a tag in brackets at the front, a tag followed by a separator, or a shared suffix. For example, 『ACE』followed by your name, or your name followed by 么, both read clearly in a match. Agree on one format with your clanmates, keep the tag identical for everyone, and let each player personalise only the name part.

How to invent your own

The most memorable names are built, not found. Start with a word that means something to you — an animal, a weather word, a favourite character, your city's nickname. Then sharpen it: swap a letter for a similar-looking one, drop the vowels for a compact look, or add a number that matters to you. Say the result out loud; if a teammate can shout it mid-fight without stumbling, it passes the voice-chat test. Finally, check the length, generate a few styled versions, and sit with your favourite for a day before spending a Rename Card on it — the guide to changing your name in PUBG Mobile explains why that pause is worth it.

What to avoid

Skip names built entirely from symbols: they look striking in a screenshot but teammates cannot pronounce them and friends cannot search for them. Avoid copying a famous player's name exactly — apart from the impersonation rules, the exact name is almost certainly taken anyway. And steer clear of anything offensive, even as a joke; a reported name can be force-changed and the Rename Card is not refunded.

Found a word you like? Head back to the generator, type it in, and compare the styles side by side — the right combination of word, font and symbol usually jumps out within a few rows.

PUBG Symbols: How to Copy and Use Special Characters

The crowns, wings and stars in stylish PUBG names are ordinary text characters from the Unicode standard — the same system that gives your phone its emoji and its alphabets. Because they are text, you can copy a symbol here and paste it into the game, no app or keyboard hack required. Tap any symbol below to copy it, then paste it around your name in the rename field. If you would rather have the whole name built for you, the stylish name generator combines these symbols with fancy fonts automatically.

Crowns and royal pieces

Crowns are the classic PUBG flex — they sit well at the front of a name or on both sides of it.

Stars

Stars are the most widely supported decoration of all, and they read clearly even at kill-feed size.

Wings, brackets and frames

Wings and brackets wrap a name and make it look finished. Use a matching pair — an opener on the left and its closer on the right.

Arrows and pointers

Arrows add direction and energy, and work especially well between a clan tag and a name.

Hearts and card suits

Hearts soften a name; suits give it a sharp, casino-style edge. Both pair well with script fonts.

Crosses, marks and gaming favourites

These are the odds and ends PUBG players reach for again and again — the 么 and 乂 marks, the katakana ツ, daggers and crosses.

How to use a copied symbol

Tap a symbol and it is copied to your clipboard with a confirmation. Then open PUBG Mobile, start the rename process described in our guide on how to change your name in PUBG, tap into the name field where you want the symbol to sit, and paste. Build the name piece by piece — symbol, name, symbol — and check the total length before confirming, because every symbol counts as a character toward the 16-character limit.

Why some symbols show as boxes

Unicode contains well over a hundred thousand characters, and no single font includes them all. PUBG Mobile's font covers the popular symbols, but an obscure character may render as an empty box or a question mark — on your screen, or only on other players' screens. The safest decorations are the ones players have used for years: crowns, stars, the ꧁༺ ༻꧂ wings and simple brackets. If a name matters to you, test the exact characters in the rename preview before the Rename Card is spent.

Tips for combining symbols

Less is usually more. One matched pair of decorations beats five different symbols fighting for attention, and a symmetrical name — the same mark on both sides — looks deliberate rather than accidental. Keep clan tags consistent: if your clan uses 『 』 brackets, everyone should use the same pair in the same place. And remember the maths from our PUBG name ideas guide: the shorter your name, the more decoration you can afford. A five-letter name wrapped in wings and a crown still fits comfortably; a fourteen-letter name barely fits at all.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the generator, Rename Cards, character limits and stylish names in general. For step-by-step help, see the guide on how to change your name in PUBG.

Is the PUBG Stylish Name generator free to use?

Yes. The generator is completely free, works instantly in your browser and needs no sign-up, download or payment. Type a name, pick a style and copy it.

How do I use a generated name in PUBG Mobile?

Copy the style you like, open PUBG Mobile, go to your Inventory, use a Rename Card, paste the name into the field and confirm. The new name appears on your profile right away.

Why does my stylish name show boxes or question marks in the game?

Boxes mean the game's font does not include that particular character. Not every Unicode symbol renders in PUBG Mobile. Pick a different style, or test the name in the rename preview before you spend a Rename Card on it. Our PUBG symbols guide explains which decorations are the safest.

How many characters can a PUBG Mobile name have?

PUBG Mobile names must be between 3 and 16 characters long, and every letter, symbol and space counts as one character. Shorter base names leave more room for crowns, wings and brackets.

Can I change my PUBG name without a Rename Card?

Your first name is free when you create your account. After that, every change needs a Rename Card. You can earn one from progress missions or buy one in the in-game shop.

Do these stylish names work in other games and apps?

Usually, yes. The styles are standard Unicode text, so they work anywhere that accepts Unicode — other games, Discord, Instagram, WhatsApp and more. How a style looks depends on the font each app uses, so a few characters may display differently from app to app.

Can I get banned for using a stylish name?

A fancy font or symbol by itself is fine. What matters is the words: names that are offensive, impersonate staff or break the game's naming rules can be reported and may be force-changed by the game. Keep the wording clean and you have nothing to worry about.

Why was my name rejected as invalid or already taken?

PUBG Mobile names must be unique, so an exact name that another player already uses cannot be repeated. Names are also rejected if they contain a character the game does not allow or a word caught by the profanity filter. Add a symbol, a number or a clan tag to make the name yours — the name ideas guide has patterns that work well.

How do I add a clan tag to my stylish name?

Generate your name first, then copy one of the bracket or prefix styles, or type your clan tag in front of the name before pasting it into the game — for example 『TAG』YourName. Just keep the total length within 16 characters.

Does this website store the name I type?

No. The generator runs entirely in your browser. The name you type is never sent to a server, saved or shared — when you close the page, it is gone.
